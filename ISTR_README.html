Why it exists

From scattered paperwork to one workflow

Mission requests used to move through inboxes and spreadsheets. ISTR gives every request one home, one owner at each stage, and one source of truth.

Before ISTR

  • Paper forms and email threads
  • No visibility into approval status
  • Manual follow-ups across departments
  • Scattered, unreportable records

With ISTR

  • Guided Service Catalog intake
  • Live status at every approval stage
  • Automated multi-level routing
  • Centralized, reportable, secure records
